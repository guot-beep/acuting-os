The first step to finding the Therapeutic Areas of the Scalp is to find the two standard lines.

1) Anterior-Posterior Midline: to find this line locate the lower border of the external occipital
protuberance with one hand and keep your pinky finger there, then with the other hand, locate
the glabella (between the two eyebrows) and put your pinky finger there. The anterior-posterior
midline follows the Du channel over the vertex of the head.

Using your thumbs, meet at a point located half-way between the glabella and the lower border
of the EOP. You can see it marked with the blue pin in the second picture (it is not on the
molding line). This point will be called the midpoint of the midline.

2) Eyebrow-Occiput Line: to find this line place one finger on the midpoint of the superior
border of the eyebrow and the other finger at the tip of the external occipital protuberance. This
line will run slightly diagonal and may or may not cross the ear depending on the anatomy of
the head.

In the picture below, you can see the blue pin indicating where the midpoint is. This is the point
that many of the measurements in the next step will be based on so take the time to get this one
correct.

Things to keep in mind…
-The molding line is not an indicator of the midline.
-The ears are funky, use them as a guide but not a measure.
-Note the blue pin in the pictures is the midpoint for this head, your head and the heads of your
patients WILL be different. You must measure every time!
