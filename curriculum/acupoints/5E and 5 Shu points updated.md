Five Shu-Transport Points Overview
 Location: between the elbows and fingers, knees and 
toes
 Analogy from well to spring, to stream, to river, to sea
 Points share common characteristics and functions
 Association with the characteristics of one of the 5 
elements
Jing – Well points
 Located at the tip of the fingers and toes (except K1)
 Qi goes out and is superficial
 Can affect the whole channel
 Function: clears heat, restores consciousness, rescues 
collapse;  treats fullness below the heart and mental 
irritation (Nan Jing)
 Associate with wood element on yin meridian
 Associate with metal element on yang meridian

--- Page 3 ---
Ying – Spring Points
 Functions: clears heat from the channel and respective 
zang fu organ
 Associate with fire element on yin meridian
 Associate with water element on yang meridian
Shu – Stream Points
 Function: treats heaviness of the body and joint pain, 
intermittent conditions
 Associate with earth element on yin meridian; Yuan-
source point
• Treats Zang organ disorders
 Associate with wood element on yang meridian
• Unblocks the channel

--- Page 4 ---
Jing – River Points
 Function: treats lung issues, dyspnea, cough, chills 
and fever, changes in voice, sinew and bone issues
 Associate with metal element on yin meridian
 Associate with fire element on yang meridian
He – Sea Points
 Locate at or near the elbow or knee
 Functions: treats stomach and intestine issues, 
counterflow of qi and diarrhea, fu organ issues
 Associate with Water element on yin meridian
 Associate with earth element on yang meridian
