The divergent channels
The chinese name is Jing bie which means “divergencies from the main
channels”
They run deeper than the primary vessels, starting at the lower end of the
main channels, connecting with the internal organs and arising to the neck.
They are distributed on the chest, abdomen and head mainly and serve as extensions of
the main meridians and supplement where the main pathway does not reach
They govern the inside of the body, running between the zang and fu, so don’t have
points
The yang divergents follow this pathway:
They arise from their associated channel, usually on the limbs, enter the yang organ,
and often the yin organ, then rejoin the main channel at the neck at the supra
clavicular fossa
The yin divergents follow this pathway:
They leave their associated channel on the limbs
The heart, lung and kidney channels enter their associated organs,
They join their paired divergent channel, then
They join the yang paired main channel at the neck
In this way, they regulate qi to and from the head and link most of the yin
and yang organs.
They do not have their own points, but they have points where the
channels meet and then exit.
UB/KID - UB10
GB/LIV - GB1
ST/SP - ST1
SI/HE - UB1
SJ/PE - SJ16
LI/LU - LI18
Functions and clinical indications:
• balance the yin and yang in the interior of the body and in the internal
organs - strengthen the connection between the yin and yang organs
• balance the flow of yin and yang between the head and the body -
especially balance ascending and descending of the yin and the yang.
3 of the 6 meeting points are window of the sky points which specifically
open the connection between the head and the body
• connect and integrate the organs internal organs - the yang divergents
flow between the yin and the yang organs, so strengthening the
connections.
● Like connecting channels, but the connecting channels
connect and integrate and balance mainly in the limbs, whereas the
divergents do this in the deeper body
• do not have their own points or pathologies

KEY POINTS ABOUT MUSCLE CHANNELS
The muscle channels in TCM are not just muscles and they are not really
channels as they have no points. They contribute to the overall circulation
of qi and blood and support the frame of the body.
In summary:
They broadly follow the course of the main meridians (except UB)
They are on the surface of the body
They do not connect with the internal organs
They all originate at the extremities
They fall in to yin/yang/ arm/leg groups - 4
They broadly follow the contours of the major muscle groups
spanning more than one muscle or just more than one channels
