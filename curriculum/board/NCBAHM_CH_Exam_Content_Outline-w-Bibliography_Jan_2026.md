## p.1

Chinese Herbology Content Outline
Effective September 1, 2026

Note to Candidate: This document serves as a guide to assist in examination preparation for
candidates who have met NCBAHMTM eligibility requirements. Below is the content outline for
the Chinese Herbology examination.

Domain I. Chinese Herbs, Herbal Combinations and Herbal Formulas (22% of Exam)
    A. Individual herbs and herbal combinations (11%)
         1. Identify herbs according to their therapeutic categories.1
         2. Identify characteristics of individual herbs (e.g., tastes, temperature, directions,
              channels entered).
         3. Identify actions and indications of individual Chinese herbs.
         4. Identify treatment strategies (Ba Fa) of individual herbs and herbal combinations
              (e.g., harmonize (He), reduce (Xiao)).
         5. Identify effects of processing (Pao Zhi) on the functions and indications of individual
              herbs (e.g., honey-fried (Mi Zhi), dry-fried (Chao)).
         6. Identify functions and indications of common herbal pairs (Dui Yao).2
         7. Identify mutual relationships of herbs (e.g., mutual accentuation (Xiang Xu), mutual
              antagonism (Xiang Wu)).
         8. Identify quality and identity of raw herbs (e.g., appearance, smell, taste).
    B. Chinese herbal formulas (11%)
         1. Identify ingredients of herbal formulas.3
         2. Identify and analyze the hierarchy within herbal formulas (e.g., chief (Jun), deputy
              (Chen)).
         3. Identify actions and indications of herbal formulas.

1 See Appendix A. Individual Chinese Herbs.
2 See Appendix B. Chinese Herbal Pairs.
3 See Appendix C. Chinese Herbal Formulas.
