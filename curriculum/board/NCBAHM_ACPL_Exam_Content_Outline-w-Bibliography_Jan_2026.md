## p.1

Acupuncture with Point Location Content Outline
Effective September 1, 2026

Note to Candidate: This document serves as a guide to assist in examination preparation for
candidates who have met NCBAHMTM eligibility requirements. Below is the content outline for
the Acupuncture with Point Location examination.

Please note: In regards to Clean Needle Technique (CNT), the Acupuncture with Point Location
module focuses on actual needling and its emergencies (e.g., needle angle and depth) in
comparison to the Biomedicine module which focuses on universal precautions and
emergency situations.

Domain I. Acupuncture Point Location (15% of Exam)
         1. Identify points on images.
         2. Identify points according to cun measurement and anatomical landmarks.

Domain II. Safety for Acupuncture Practice (15% of Exam)
         1. Describe risks and benefits of acupuncture, moxibustion, and adjunctive therapies.
         2. Recognize risk factors for individual patients (e.g., pregnancy, pacemaker, blood
              thinners, diabetes, hypoglycemia).
         3. Recognize and manage adverse events (e.g., burns, pneumothorax, bruising,
              hemorrhage, needle shock, stuck needle).
         4. Comply with Clean Needle Technique (CNT) standards and procedures.
         5. Apply infection control procedures to acupuncture practice (e.g., CDC's Standard
              Precautions, OSHA standards).

Domain III. Treatment Planning (40% of Exam)
    A. Selection of treatment methods (25%)
         1. Establish a treatment plan with appropriate treatment methods (e.g., acupuncture,
              cupping, moxibustion, combination of modalities).

Domain IV. Treatment Techniques (30% of Exam)
    A. Acupuncture (15%)
         1. Identify indications, precautions and contraindications for acupuncture (e.g.,
              pregnancy, organ damage, inflamed or non-intact skin).
         2. Apply appropriate patient positioning and draping procedures.
         3. Select needles to achieve desired treatment goals (e.g., length, gauge, filiform, or
              other types of needles).
         4. Apply appropriate needling techniques:
                   a. Insertion (e.g., angle, depth, stretching skin)
                   b. Manipulation (e.g., arrival of Qi, reinforcing, reducing, lifting, thrusting)
                   c. Retention time
                   d. Removal (e.g., order, speed, pressure)
         5. Apply myofascial trigger point (MTrP) techniques (e.g., local twitch response (LTR),
              dry needling, motor points).
         6. Adjust needling technique based on patient's response (e.g., stimulation method,
              intensity, needle retention time).
    B. Moxibustion (5%)
         1. Identify indications, precautions and contraindications for moxibustion.
         2. Select the appropriate moxibustion technique (e.g., direct, indirect, warming
              needle).
         3. Adjust the moxibustion technique based on patient's response.

Acupuncture with Point Location Examination Suggested Bibliography

The Content Outline is the primary resource for studying for this examination. The purpose of this
Bibliography is only to provide the candidate with suggested resources to utilize in preparation for the
examination. Candidates should feel free to consider other resources that cover the material in the
Content Outline.

There is no single text recommended by NCBAHMTM. All NCBAHMTM examination modules reflect
practice in the United States as determined by the most recent job analysis.

NCBAHMTM's item writers and examination development committee members frequently use the
following texts as resources; however, the sources used are not limited to the books listed here. The
NCBAHMTM does not endorse any third-party study/preparation guide.
