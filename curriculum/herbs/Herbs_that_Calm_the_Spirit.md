Suān Zăo Rén 酸 枣 仁
• Semen Zizyphi Spinosae
• English: sour jujube seed, zizyphus
• Properties: sweet, sour, neutral
• Channels: Heart. Liver
Actions and Indications
Actions: Nourishes the Heart yin, augments the Liver blood, and quiets the spirit.
Indications: for irritability, insomnia, and palpitations with anxiety due to either blood deficiency or yin
deficiency
Shen disturbance due to liver yin deficiency with deficient heat: Zhi Mu, Fu Ling (suan zao ren tang)
Shen disturbance due to heart and kidney deficiency: Sheng Di, Xuan Shen, Dang Gui, Bai Zi Ren
Actions: Prevents abnormal sweating; mild astringent to stop sweating
Indications: for both spontaneous sweating and night sweats: Dang Shen, Wu Wei Zi, Shan Zhu Yu
Main Actions:
• Nourishes the heart and liver and calms Shen
• Prevents sweating
Dosage: 10-30 grams in decoction. Crush before decocting to increase the extraction of active
constituents. As powder, take 1.5-3 grams before bedtime.
Unprocessed Suan Zao Ren is cooler and better for insomnia with heat signs. The dry-fried form is
warmer and better for calming the Shen and relieving insomnia.
Cautions:
Suan Zao Ren is contraindicated in patients with insomnia caused by excess heat or by hot phlegm
accumulation.
Use with caution for patients who have diarrhea or an exterior condition.
Use of this herb may be associated with drowsiness and sedation.
It should be used with caution during pregnancy, as it stimulates contraction of the uterus.
