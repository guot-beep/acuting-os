         Mnemonics for the 18 Incompatibilities in Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM)
The 18 Incompatibilities (Shi Ba Fan or ) in TCM are pairs of herbs that are
traditionally believed to be incompatible and potentially harmful when used together in a
formula. It's crucial for TCM practitioners to remember these combinations. While no
single universally accepted mnemonic exists, here are some common approaches and
ideas for creating one:

                                   Mnemonic approaches

You can use various techniques to remember these incompatibilities:
1. Acronyms
Create acronyms for each group, though they may not always be easy to pronounce or
recall. For example, for Gan Cao, you could use GDYHHZ based on the first letters of the
pinyin.
2. Acrostics or Sentences
Develop a sentence where the first letter of each word corresponds to the first letter of the
incompatible herb. For instance, for Gan Cao, a sentence could be "Great Success Doesn't
Just Happen Here, Zealously".
3. Visualization and Storytelling
Visualizing scenarios or creating stories can be a powerful method. You can imagine a
story for each group of incompatible herbs to make them more memorable.
4. Combining techniques
Combining acronyms or acrostics with visualization can also be an effective approach.
Important Note
While mnemonics are helpful, understanding the rationale behind these incompatibilities
is crucial. The pairings are based on the traditional properties and actions of the herbs and
their potential interactions.
