                     Herbs That Drain Dampness

� Promote normal urination, increase urinary output, and eliminate bodily
    accumulation of water and dampness.

� Sweet and/or bland and neutral or cool herbs are commonly used to treat
    accumulation of water, dampness, and phlegm.

� Bitter or sweet and cold herbs drain damp-heat, and are commonly used to treat
    dysuria, infections in the genitourinary tract, or stones in the urinary tract.

� Lung, Spleen and Kidney control the circulation of water.
� When these organs fail to carry out their normal functions, disease occurs.
� Cautions: herbs that regulate water and drain dampness may deplete yin and body

    fluids.
