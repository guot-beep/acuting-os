Materia Medica III – Yang-Tonifying Herbs
Overview
Yang-tonifying herbs warm and supplement Kidney yang, assist the Spleen and Heart
yang, replenish essence (Jing), strengthen sinews and bones, and support fertility, growth,
and recovery from chronic deficiency. Many also dispel cold-damp from the channels.

• Strong yang tonics are contraindicated in exterior or excess heat.
• Several herbs are hot or toxic → duration and dosing are exam-relevant.

Lù róng 鹿茸
Cornu Cervi Pantotrichum | Deer antler velvet
Nature & Flavor: Sweet, salty, warm
Channels: Kidney, Liver
Primary Actions:
1. Strongly tonifies Kidney Yang and replenishes Jing
2. Nourishes Blood; strengthens sinews and bones
3. Regulates Chong & Ren; stabilizes Dai Mai
4. Heals chronic Yin sores and boils
Key Indications:
• Severe Kidney Yang deficiency: impotence, infertility, cold extremities

• Developmental delay, weak bones, delayed fontanel closure
• Chong/Ren deficiency cold leading to heavy uterine bleeding or infertility (cold

Dosage:
• 1–3 g, powder, decocted separately

Strongest Yang tonic; short-term, severe deficiency only
