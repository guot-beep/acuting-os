Herbs that Regulate Qi
• Herbs that regulate qi function to promote normal circulation of qi, correct reversed
flow of qi, and eliminate qi stagnation.
• Most of the herbs that regulate qi are aromatic and warm, with acrid and bitter
properties.
• The most important organs involved in the qi mechanism are the Lungs, which
govern qi; the Liver, which governs dredging and draining; the Spleen, which governs
transformation and transportation; and the Stomach, which governs receiving and
taking in.
• These herbs are rarely used alone; instead, they are often combined with others
based on the specific nature of the disorder.
• The herbs that regulate qi are drying in nature and should be used with caution for
patients with qi and yin deficiency or yin deficiency caused by febrile disorders.
