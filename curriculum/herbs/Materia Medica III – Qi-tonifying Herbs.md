Tonic Herbs – Overview
Tonic herbs strengthen the body’s vital substances, including qi, blood, yin, and yang.
They are used to restore physiological function, support constitutional weakness, enhance
immune resistance, and promote recovery from chronic illness.
Major Categories of Tonic Herbs
• Qi tonifying herbs: Strengthen the Spleen and Lung to improve transformation,
transportation, and respiration.
‑
• Yang tonifying herbs: Warm and supplement Kidney yang and may secondarily
support Spleen and Heart yang.
‑
• Blood tonifying herbs: Nourish and replenish blood, primarily affecting the Liver,
Heart, and Spleen.
‑
• Yin tonifying herbs: Nourish yin and generate fluids, most commonly targeting the
Kidney and Liver, with secondary effects on the Lung and Stomach.
‑
Common Deficiency Combinations
• Qi and yang deficiency
• Qi and blood deficiency
• Qi and yin deficiency
• Yin and blood deficiency
• Yin and yang deficiency
General Cautions for Tonic Herbs
• Qi tonics are sweet and cloying and may cause dampness or stagnation in the
Middle Jiao.
• Yang tonics are warm and drying and may damage yin or aggravate heat.
• Blood and yin tonics may be heavy or greasy, impairing digestion.
Pharmacological Correlates
• Immunomodulatory
• Adaptogenic
• Endocrine regulating
• Metabolic
‑
• Cardiovascular supportive
