Materia Medica III � Astringent Herbs (Stabilize & Bind)

Overview

Astringent herbs bind, retain, and restrain leakage of qi, blood, essence, and body fluids.
They are used to prevent further loss, not to restore what is already depleted. Appropriate
use requires confirmation that pathogenic factors have been cleared.

General Characteristics

Attribute    Description
Taste        Sour, astringent

Temperature Varies
Channels Lung, Heart, Liver, Kidney, Large Intestine

Core Actions Restrain leakage; stabilize essence; stop discharge

Global Cautions

    � Do not use when exterior pathogens, damp-heat, food stagnation, or excess heat
         are present.

    � Premature use may trap pathogens.
