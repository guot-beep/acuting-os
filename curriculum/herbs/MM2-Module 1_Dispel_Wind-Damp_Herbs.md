Notes: Han Fang Ji is stronger in promoting urination and reducing edema, Mu Fang Ji is stronger
in expelling wind-damp and alleviating pain.
Dosage: 5-10 grams in decoction.
Cautions:
Guang (Mu) Fang Ji is Toxic, Han Fang Ji is bitter and cold; it damages the middle jiao, and should
be used with caution in patients with yin deficiency, no signs of damp-heat, or in patients with
deficiency and cold of the Spleen and Stomach.
Toxicology:
Guang Fang Ji is toxic, and its use should be avoided. The toxic component is aristolochic acid. It
has been shown to affect the liver, kidneys, and adrenal glands.
Herb-drug interaction: Fen Fang Ji has a diuretic effect; concurrent use with diuretic drugs may
lead to increased elimination of water and or electrolytes. Examples of diuretics include Lasix,
Bumex, and Demadex.)
