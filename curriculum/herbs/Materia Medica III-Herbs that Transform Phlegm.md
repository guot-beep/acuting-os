Materia Medica III-Herbs that Transform Phlegm

Overview

Phlegm-transforming herbs resolve phlegm (tan), redirect rebellious Lung and Stomach qi,
relieve cough and wheezing, and soften nodules or masses. Phlegm is both a pathological
product and a pathogenic factor, frequently complicating chronic disease.

These herbs are subdivided clinically into: - Cold phlegm vs. hot phlegm - Wind-phlegm
obstructing the channels - Phlegm nodules and masses

General Characteristics

Attribute Description

Taste  Bitter, acrid

Temperature Warm (cold phlegm) or cold (hot phlegm)

Channels Lung, Spleen (primary)

Core Actions Transform phlegm; stop cough; redirect qi

GLOBAL FUNCTION

Used when phlegm presents as:
    � cough/wheeze
    � nausea from phlegm obstruction
    � nodules, goiter, scrofula
    � wind-phlegm stroke patterns
    � phlegm-heat agitation

GLOBAL CAUTIONS

    � Avoid in Yin deficiency dryness (warm drying herbs)
    � Toxic herbs must be processed (Ban Xia, Tian Nan Xing)
    � Use cold phlegm herbs cautiously in cold-deficiency patients
    � Do not use during the early stages of measles or rashes.
    � Stimulating phlegm herbs may worsen bleeding (e.g., hemoptysis).

Ban Xia 

Scientific name: Rhizoma Pinelliae
Nature/Flavor: Acrid, warm, toxic
Channels: LU, SP, ST

Primary actions:

    � Dries damp; transforms phlegm
    � Redirects rebellious qi downward
    � Stops vomiting
    � Dissipates nodules

Top indications:

    � Phlegm-damp cough with copious sputum
    � Nausea/vomiting from phlegm obstruction
    � Chest fullness, Globus hystericus
    � Phlegm nodules

Dosage:

    � 5�10 g (processed only)
    � Must be processed (ginger + alumen)
    � Forms:

             o Qing Ban Xia  phlegm-damp cough
             o Fa Ban Xia  wind-phlegm
             o Jiang Ban Xia  nausea/vomiting

Contraindications:

    � Toxic unprocessed
    � Avoid:

             o Yin deficiency dry cough
             o Heat sore throat
             o Bleeding disorders
             o Hot phlegm
    � Incompatible with Wu Tou
    � Overdose  neuro-oral toxicity, respiratory depression

Exam pearl:

Essential herb for phlegm and is the yao yao for phlegm of all types.
    � Treats both:
             o phlegm-damp cough
             o phlegm-induced nausea/vomiting
    � Dries from source + branch
