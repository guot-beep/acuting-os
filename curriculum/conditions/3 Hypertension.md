Hypertension

Hypertension defined as artery systolic blood pressure at rest of >140 mm Hg and/or diastolic blood pressure of >90 mmHg, affects an estimated 640 million persons worldwide, including approximately 50 million Americans.

High blood pressure is classified as primary hypertension and secondary hypertension.

In Western Medicine  
Etiology & Pathology
Primary Hypertension
The exact cause of primary high blood pressure is not known. Below several factors and conditions may play a role in its development, including:
Smoking
Lack of physical activity
Too much salt in the diet
Too many fatty foods
Too much alcohol consumption
Stress

	Lonely
Older age
Family history of high blood pressure  
Environment
Chronic kidney disease
Adrenal and thyroid disorders

Secondary Hypertension
Kidney disease
Adrenal glands
Pregnancy
