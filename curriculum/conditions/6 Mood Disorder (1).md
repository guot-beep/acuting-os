Mood Disorder

Depressive disorder is a mood disorder in which feeling of sadness, loss, anger, or frustration interfering with daily life for weeks or longer.

Etiology & pathology in western medicine  
The exact cause of depression is unknown, it is believed that it is the chemical changes in the brain, it may be due to a problem with the genes or may be triggered by certain stressful events or a combination of both.

Below are some possible causes
Heredity, alcohol, drug abuse, sleeping disorder, stress, certain medications or certain medical conditions

Manifestations:
The patients see everything in a negative way
Agitation, restlessness and irritability, anger
Feeling isolation
Feeling hopeless, helpless, worthless, guilty and self-hate
hallucinations
Fatigue
Loss of interest or pleasure in activities that were once enjoyed
Sudden change in appetite
Weight changes
Constipation
Pain
Hurting themselves
Thoughts of suicide
Poor sleeping
Poor concentration
Poor sexual drive
It often occurs with PMS
It occurs in not only adult, children and teens as well

When to call 911?
If your patients have thoughts of suicide
Harming him/herself or others
Suicide hotline 1-800-273-TALK (8255) or 1-800-SUICIDE (7842433) or 1-800-999-9999

When to call the PCP?
Symptoms of postpartum depression
Hallucinations
Any symptoms of depression for longer than 2 weeks
