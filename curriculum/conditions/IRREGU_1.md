Irregular  Menstruation

Irregular menstruation includes -

Early Menstruation
Menstruation earlier than due time by 7 to 8 days,  or even twice a month, is regarded as early menstruation.

Late Menstruation
Menstruation later than due time by 8 to 9 days, or even every 40 to 50 days is considered as late menstruation.

Irregular Menstruation
Referring to sometimes early, sometimes late menstruation.

Case Study

1.  A 31 y-o quick-talking woman comes in with complaint of menstruation every 2 weeks.
     She has been menstruating irregularly since her menarche, most early menstruation.
Last year she started a new job which stressed her out.  For six months she has bled heavily twice a month.  She said that the menstruation appears purple red, thick with dark clots.  Premenstrually, the patient feels lower back soreness and abdominal distension, irritable, short-tempered, dry and bitter taste in the mouth, stifling sensation in the chest.
