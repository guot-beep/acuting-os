Dizziness and Vertigo

Dizziness includes two different feelings
Lightheadedness is a feeling about to faint
Vertigo causes a spinning

Manifestations of vertigo:
A sensation of motion
Nausea or vomiting
Abnormal eye movements
Difficulty speaking
Difficulty walking
Symptoms can be constant or episodic
Duration can be from minutes to hours
The cause of onset can be due to a movement

When to refer to ER?
Double vision
Headache
Weakness limbs
Difficulty walking
Difficulty controlling the arms
Difficulty speaking
Abnormal eye movements
Consciousness not acting appropriately

Manifestations
Excessive Condition:
Liver Yang Uprising:
Main Symptoms:
Excess: Dizziness/vertigo, aggravated by stress, anger; headache, migraine, easy anger, bitter taste, insomnia with dream disturb; blurry vision, relieved by rest
Deficiency: Dizziness and vertigo, Lower back pain, tinnitus, fatigue, dull mind
Tongue: red body, thin yellow coating; peeled coating; big tongue with little or no teeth mark
Pulse: wiry, rapid
Treatment Principles: Calm liver to suppress yang; Tonify kidney yin
Point Prescription:  Liv3, GB20, UB18, UB23, Liv2, GB43, SP6, KD3

Phlegm Obstruction:
Main Symptoms: Dizziness/vertigo feels like being on a boat, or feels like the world is spinning around, even in bed (feel like the bed is spinning) or eye closed; Symptoms may be triggered by movement of the head or strong smells; heavy head, chest distention, fatigue, nausea
Tongue: white greasy coat, big body
Pulse: soft, slippery
Treatment Principles:  dampness, resolve phlegm, regulate Spleen, stop dizziness
Point Prescription:  ST8, P6, Ren12, SP9, SP6, ST40

Blood Stagnation:
Main Symptoms: Dizziness/vertigo due to external injury in the head or dizziness/vertigo with no cure after treatment for a long time; headache in a fixed area or prickling pain with no response to conventional treatment; Dark complexion and lips, insomnia, lassitude, palpitation
Tongue: dark purple tongue or with ecchymosis
Pulse: choppy, uneven
Treatment Principles:  Remove blood stasis, promote blood circulation; Clear channels, induce resuscitation
Point Prescription: DU20, GB20, UB17, SP10, LI4, SP6

Deficiency Condition:
Qi and Blood Deficiency:
Main Symptoms: Dizziness/vertigo, easily occurring or aggravated when the patient is tired; Pale and dim complexion, pale lips and nails, lassitude, shortness breath, palpitation, insomnia, dreaminess, forgetfulness, poor memory, spots before the eyes, poor appetite, poor digestion
Tongue: pale tongue, thin coat
Pulse: thready, weak
Treatment Principles:  Tonify spleen, nourish heart
Point Prescription:  Du20, UB15, UB20, UB17, Ren4, ST36, HT7, P6, Si Shen Cong

Kidney Essence Deficiency:
Main Symptoms: Dizziness/vertigo that is worse with sex, overexertion, and late night; Lower back and knee soreness or weakness, heel pain, listlessness, tinnitus, poor sexual function
Yin deficiency-Feverish sensation in the chest, palms and soles, night sweat, nocturnal emission, irregular menstruation
Yang deficiency-Aversion to cold, cold pain in the waist and knees, cold of the genitals, impotence
Tongue: Yin Xu- small red tongue, scanty coat; Yang Xu- pale flabby tongue
Pulse: Yin Xu- rapid, thready; Yang Xu- deep, soft
Treatment Principles:  Yin deficiency–nourish yin, reinforce kidney; Yang deficiency–restore yang, warm kidney
Point Prescription: DU20, Si Shen Cong, GB20, UB10, UB17, UB23, DU4, KD3, GB39, Tai Yang
