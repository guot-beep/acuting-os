Myopia

Myopia, also known as nearsightedness or close-sightedness, refers to a condition in which a patient can clearly see objects that are close but not objects that are far away (obscurely) due to hypofunction of lens flexibility.  It mostly occurs in children or teenagers.
Usually, it can be completely corrected with glasses, contact lenses, or surgery.

In TCM, this disorder called " enable to see the near and scare to look at the distant. "

Etiology and Pathogenesis

Myopia occurs when the eye is too long for the focus point of the cornea.  Light rays that normally focus on the retina focus in front of it.  This causes images to appear blurry.  Some experts think that myopia is something you are born with.  Some think it is caused by the stress of too much reading, close work, or computer use.  It is likely that both factors play a role in this disorder.  Myopia may also be caused by a cornea that is too steep.  In older age, myopia can be caused by cataracts.
