Constipation

Constipation refers to a condition difficulty during defecation excrete, the sensation of incomplete bowel evacuation and infrequent bowel movements typically three times or fewer per week.

The Rome III criteria: The definition of constipation must be based on at least 2 of the below conditions in the duration within 12 weeks over the period of 12 months.
<3 bowel movements/week
>25% hard stool
>25% the sensation of incomplete bowel evacuation
>25% difficulty bowel movement
>25% Manual maneuvers to facilitate
Insufficient criteria for IBS

Gender: F>M
Age: Older>younger
It is not an infectious disease
