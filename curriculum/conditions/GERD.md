## Definition 定義

Gastroesophageal reflux disease — chronic condition where gastric acid flows back into the esophagus due to **lower esophageal sphincter (LES) incompetence**, causing mucosal irritation.

胃食道逆流疾病 — 因**下食道括約肌（LES）功能不全**，胃酸持續逆流進入食道，造成黏膜刺激與損傷。

---

## Pathophysiology 病理機制

```
LES incompetence 下食道括約肌鬆弛
        ↓
Acid reflux into esophagus 胃酸逆流
        ↓
Mucosal irritation / erosion 黏膜刺激與糜爛
        ↓ (if chronic)
Barrett Esophagus ⭐ — intestinal metaplasia 腸化生
        ↓
Adenocarcinoma risk 腺癌風險
```

---

## Risk Factors 危險因子

| Factor | Notes |
|---|---|
| Obesity 肥胖 | Increases intra-abdominal pressure |
| Hiatal hernia 食道裂孔疝 | Allows acid to reflux more easily |
| Pregnancy 懷孕 | Hormonal + mechanical pressure |
| Smoking 抽菸 | Relaxes LES |
| Alcohol 飲酒 | Relaxes LES |
| Spicy / fatty food 辛辣油膩 | Triggers reflux |

## Signs & Symptoms 症狀與徵象

| Symptom | Chinese | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Heartburn** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | 胃灼熱 | Worse after meals, lying flat, bending |
| **Regurgitation** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | 胃酸逆流 | Sour/bitter taste 酸苦味 |
| Dysphagia 🚩 | 吞嚥困難 | Rule out stricture or malignancy |
| Chest pain | 胸痛 | Must rule out cardiac cause first |
| Chronic cough | 慢性咳嗽 | Extra-esophageal / atypical GERD |
| Hoarseness | 聲音沙啞 | Laryngeal irritation |

## Diagnosis 診斷

| Test | When to use | Chinese |
|---|---|---|
| Clinical diagnosis | Most cases — typical heartburn + regurgitation | 臨床診斷為主 |
| **Endoscopy** ⭐ | Alarm symptoms, no PPI response, Barrett screening | 內視鏡：有警訊時 |
| pH monitoring | Gold standard for acid exposure quantification | 24小時pH監測：黃金標準 |
| Esophageal manometry | Rule out achalasia | 食道測壓 |

---

## Treatment 治療

| Level | Treatment | Chinese |
|---|---|---|
| **1st line** ⭐ | **PPI** (proton pump inhibitor) + lifestyle | **質子幫浦抑制劑** + 生活改善 |
| 2nd line | H2 blockers 組織胺H2受體阻斷劑 | Milder cases |
| Symptom relief | Antacids 制酸劑 | Short-term only |
| Surgical | Nissen fundoplication | Severe / refractory cases |

**Lifestyle modifications 生活改善:**
- Elevate head of bed 抬高床頭
- Avoid triggers: coffee, alcohol, chocolate, fatty/spicy food
- No eating 2–3 hrs before bed 睡前2-3小時禁食
- Weight loss if obese

| Esophageal stricture | Chronic scarring → dysphagia |
| Esophagitis | Mucosal inflammation / erosion |

## Red Flags — Refer Immediately 🚩

> 🚩 **Progressive dysphagia** 吞嚥困難 → possible stricture or malignancy → endoscopy
> 🚩 **Weight loss + GERD symptoms** → rule out esophageal adenocarcinoma
> 🚩 **Hematemesis / melena** 吐血/黑便 → GI bleed → ER
> 🚩 **No PPI response after 8 weeks** → endoscopy to rule out Barrett or cancer
