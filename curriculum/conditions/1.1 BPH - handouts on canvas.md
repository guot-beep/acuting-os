Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia
Prostate Location: The prostate is a walnut-sized gland located between the bladder and the penis.
Prostate Function: The prostate secretes fluid that nourishes and protects sperm.
Benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) is an enlarged prostate gland.
Benign prostatic hyperplasia is also known as benign prostatic hypertrophy.

Etiology and Pathogenesis:
In western medicine: The presence of DHT stimulates growth factors in the prostate, leading to hyperplasia with the resulting increase in size.

Prostate Tests
Digital rectal examination (DRE)
Prostate-specific antigen (PSA)
fPSA (free, unbound PSA)
Prostate ultrasound
Prostate biopsy
When PSA is somewhere above 2.5 ng/ml:
(1)PSA Above 10 ng/mlbiopsy
(2) Between 2.5-10 ng/ml
a.Normal DRE (digital rectal exam)fPSA test
b.Abnormal DREbiopsy
(3) fPSA test (good news = high % fPSA)
a.if less than or equal to 7% free PSA biopsy
b.if between 7-15% fPSA, debatable, as some will recommend biopsy now, while others recommend
            waiting 6-12 months for another test.  The reason is that most men with prostate cancer have fPSA
            levels below 15%.
            c.if 15-25% wait one year for another PSA and fPSA test.
            -- Allows PSA velocity to be determined
            -- Allows refinement of fPSA reading
            d.if above 25% likely benign (i.e. BPH) Repeat PSA and DRE at annual exam.

Clinical Manifestation:
In Western Medicine
Common BPH Symptoms
Symptoms of this condition usually start after the age of 50. They can include:
Passing urine often, especially at night
Feeling that the bladder has not fully emptied
A strong or sudden urge to urinate
A weak or slow urine stream
Stopping and starting again several times while passing urine
Pushing or straining to begin passing urine
Hematuria
White color discharge at the Urethral meatus at the end of urination or during bowl movement
Pain at the lumbosacral, low abdomen, groin, pelvic, inguinal region, and at the medial aspect of thigh and at the testes
Low libido, premature ejaculation, erectile dysfunction, seminal emission, and painful ejaculation
Dizziness, fatigue, excessive dream, insomnia

Severe Symptoms
At their worst, BPH symptoms can lead to:
A weak bladder
Backflow of urine causing bladder or kidney infections
Complete block in the flow of urine
Kidney failure
