Chronic Fatigue Syndrome

It refers to a group of symptoms characterized by severe continued tiredness that is not relieved by rest and is not directly caused by other medical conditions lasting more than 6 months.

Etiology and Pathology
In Western Medicine
This syndrome is not clearly understood in western medicine, it is believed to be due to:
Epstein-Barr virus or human herpes virus-6 (HHV-6)
Inflammation in the nervous system, because of a faulty immune system response
