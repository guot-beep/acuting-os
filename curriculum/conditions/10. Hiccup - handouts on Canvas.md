Hiccup

A hiccup is an involuntary contraction of the diaphragm that may repeat several times per minute.

Etiology and Pathology
In Western Medicine  
Rapid eating
Carbonated beverages, alcohol
Certain drugs

In Chinese Medicine
Excessive Conditions
Stomach Cold:
Symptoms: deep, long and forceful; cramping pain in stomach
Tongue: white moist coat
Pulse: slow
Treatment Principle: warm middle Jiao, expel Cold, and descend Stomach Qi
Acupuncture: Ren 22, P6, BL 17, ST 36, Ren 12, LR 13, BL 20, 21, Ren 4, ST 21

Stomach Fire:
Symptoms: loud, clear and forceful; mouth ulcers
Treatment: red, with yellow coat
Pulse: slippery rapid,
Treatment Principle: clear and purge Stomach Fire, descend Stomach Qi
Acupuncture: Ren22, BL17, ST36, P6, ST25, ST44, LI 4, SP 4 & PC 4, ST 43

Qi and Phlegm Stagnation:
Symptoms: continuous Hiccups, vomiting, induced by stress or anxiety.
Tongue: thin greasy coat
Pulse: wiry slippery
Treatment Principle: regulate Qi, transform Phlegm, harmonize Stomach Qi
Acupuncture: Ren22, P6, ST36, BL17, Liv14, GB 43, ST 40, GB 34, Du 20, GB 20

Deficiency Conditions
Stomach Yang Xu:
Symptoms: weak and deep low tone sound; cold limbs and body. Fatigue, poor appetite
Tongue: white coat
Pulse: deep thin,
Treatment Principle: warm mid Jiao, harmonize Stomach Qi
Acupuncture: Ren22, P6, ST36, UB17, Ren12, UB20, UB21, Ren 6, Ren 4 & 8

Stomach Yin Xu:
Symptoms: short, quick; abdominal pain, thirst, dry mouth, tongue and lips
Tongue: red, cracked, scanty dry coat
Pulse: thin rapid
Treatment Principle: nourish Stomach Yin and fluids, descend Stomach Qi
Acupuncture: Ren22, P6, ST 36, UB 17, 21, Ren 12, Kd 3 or 6, Ren 10, SP 3

Western Medicine Treatment:
Treatment on the underlying disorder if it is the secondary to some other causes

General Acupuncture Treatments:
  Auricular – mid ear, shenmen, subcortex, stomach, liver, spleen
Scalp acupuncture – Stomach region, chest region
