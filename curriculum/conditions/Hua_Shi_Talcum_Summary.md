Huá Shí (Talcum) – One-Page Summary
Pharmaceutical Name: Talcum
English Name: Talc / Soapstone
Part Used: Mineral (powder form or decoction pieces)
Properties:
  - Taste: Sweet, Bland
  - Temperature: Cold
  - Channels Entered: Stomach, Urinary Bladder
Key Functions
- Clears Heat and promotes urination
- Clears Summer-Heat and relieves irritability and thirst
- Absorbs moisture and treats skin lesions (topical use)
Indications
- Damp-Heat in the Bladder: Burning, painful urination, dark scanty urine
- Summer-Heat with Dampness: Fever, restlessness, urinary difficulty
- Topical: Eczema, rashes, oozing lesions
Preparation & Dosage
- Internal Use: 9–15 g in decoction; wrap in cheesecloth before boiling to avoid grit
- Topical Use: Apply as fine powder for damp skin conditions
Common Combinations
- Huá Shí + Che Qián Zǐ: Enhances urination for Damp-Heat Lin syndrome
- Huá Shí + Xi Guā or Lu Dòu: Clears Summer-Heat and thirst
- Huá Shí + Yì Yǐ Rén: Clears Damp-Heat and reduces fever
Cautions & Contraindications
- Avoid in Spleen deficiency with loose stool
- Use caution during pregnancy
- Do not confuse medical Huá Shí with industrial or cosmetic talc
Raw vs. Cooked Talc
Type
	Use
	Key Note

Raw Talc
	Internal – clears Heat, promotes urination
	Medicinal grade; wrap before decoction

Cooked Talc
	External – cosmetic powder only
	Not for internal use

Summary
Huá Shí is a sweet, bland, cold mineral that clears Heat, drains Dampness, and releases Summer-Heat. It is used for urinary difficulty and heat-related skin problems. Always use Raw Talc for medicine and wrap before boiling.
