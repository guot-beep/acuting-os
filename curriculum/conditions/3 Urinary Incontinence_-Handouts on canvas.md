Urinary Incontinence

Urinary Incontinence refers to the situation in which the urinary system is accidental urine leakage while in conscious condition

Nocturnal Unuresis refers to the situation in which the urinary system is accidental urine leakage while in unconscious condition during sleeping. Usually it is related to young children.

Etiology and Pathogenesis:
In western medicine: The causes of urinary Incontinence are:
1. Stress Incontinence – happens when lifting objects
2. Urgent Incontinence - an urge to urinate but can't make it in time
3. Overflow Incontinence - happens when the bladder doesn't empty
4. Total incontinence - happens when leaking urine
5. Involuntary Incontinence: the person unaware of the incontinence

Clinical Manifestation:
In Western Medicine
The most common sign of urinary incontinence is leaking urine from the bladder. Other signs will depend on the type of urinary incontinence.
Weak constitution. Long term debilitating disease
Shock to developing child or fetus
Old age: Physiological change
Excessive Sexual activity: kidney essence deficiency
Child birth:  uses up essence to give birth
Chronic cough: the repeated act of cough
