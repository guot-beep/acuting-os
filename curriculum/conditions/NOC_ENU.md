                                         Nocturnal  Enuresis

Nocturnal enuresis ( bedwetting ) refers to urination during sleeping in those kids over three years old.
In TCM, enuresis is associated with lung, spleen, kidney and UB.

Etiology and Pathogenesis

Weak constitution
Kidney Qi deficiency
Lung Qi and spleen Qi deficiency

Differentiation

Lower Jiao Cold & Deficiency

Lung and Spleen Qi Deficiency

Treatment

Warm and strengthen kidney Qi by reinforcing techniques and moxibustion on Ren, Sp, UB and Lu points.
