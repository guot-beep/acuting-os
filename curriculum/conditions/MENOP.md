                                      Menopausal  Syndrome

Menopausal syndrome refers to a group of symptoms and signs coming up before or after menopause.

Typically including emotional lability, irritability, listlessness, sweating, dizziness, palpitation, insomnia, five center burn, tinnitus, forgetfulness, formication, lumbar soreness, thirst, fatigue, and even in extreme case psychosis.  The severity and duration of these symptoms may vary greatly from case to case.

Etiology and Pathogenesis

Kidney Qi declining, Chong & Ren becoming empty
Imbalance between Yin and Yang

Differentiation

1.   Kidney Yin Deficiency

2.   Kidney Yang Deficiency

      Acupoints mainly selected from K, Lr, Ren, Du and Back Shu.
      Tonifying and reducing techniques may be used at the same time for Kidney Yin Deficiency;  Tonifying technique and moxibustion are advisable for Kidney Yang Deficiency.
