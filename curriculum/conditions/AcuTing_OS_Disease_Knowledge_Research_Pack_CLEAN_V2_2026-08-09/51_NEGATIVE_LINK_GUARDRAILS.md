# Negative-Link and False-Equivalence Guardrails

Do not author the following kinds of false identity:

```text
hypertension IS dizziness
stroke IS Liver Wind
MI IS 胸痺
asthma IS 哮病
COPD IS 喘證
GERD IS 吞酸
diabetes IS 消渴
psoriasis IS 白疕
Bell palsy IS 面癱
trigeminal neuralgia IS 面痛
depression IS 鬱證
cancer IS 癥瘕 or 虛勞
hearing loss IS 耳鳴耳聾
menopause IS 絕經前後諸證
```

Preferred semantics:

```text
strong clinical association
common TCM presentation
possible contextual association
differential context
emergency differential context
unsupported / do not link
```

Any relation that would erase subtype, cause, emergency status or normal-life-stage boundaries should be rejected or weakened.
