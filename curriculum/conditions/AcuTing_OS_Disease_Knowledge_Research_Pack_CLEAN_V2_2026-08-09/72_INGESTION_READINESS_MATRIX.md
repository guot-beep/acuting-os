## Stop conditions

```text
candidate ID conflicts with existing identity/alias
TDIS path authority unresolved
relation endpoint missing
validator requires unknown field semantics
exact ICD specificity cannot be supported
TCM source does not support staged mechanism claim
diff unexpectedly deletes or rewrites unrelated canonical content
```
