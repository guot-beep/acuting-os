Hemorrhoids

Hemorrhoids are painful, swollen veins in the lower portion of the rectum or anus.

Zangfu involved in hemorrhoids: Liver and GB, spleen and stomach, kidney, lung

Western Medicine Etiology and Pathology
The exact cause of symptomatic hemorrhoids is unknown.

Hemorrhoids may be caused by:
Constipation
Pregnancy and after childbirth
Sitting for long periods of time

Hemorrhoids may be Internal and External:
Internal hemorrhoids occur just inside the anus, at the beginning of the rectum, or it is located above the pectinate line
External hemorrhoids occur at the anal opening and may hang outside the anus or it is located below the pectinate line.

TCM Etiology and Pathology
Mental Stress
Improper diet
Prolonged sitting，standing
Chronic constipation or diarrhea

General Symptoms and Signs:
Anal itching
Anal ache or pain
Bright red blood on toilet tissue, stool, or in the toilet bowl
Pain during bowel movements
One or more hard tender lumps near the anus

Classification of Hemorrhoids
1st degree: small, non-protruding hemorrhoids, characterized mainly by bleeding, bleeding stops spontaneously after defecation.
2nd degree: larger hemorrhoids which protrude upon defecation, but are easily reducible or retract spontaneously; bleeding can also be a feature.
3rd degree: large hemorrhoids which protrude with increase abdominal or with walking, standing and defecation; may require manual reduction to alleviate discomfort, or may be difficult to reduce; bleeding is a feature.
4th degree: larger hemorrhoids which protrude constantly may protrude spontaneously easily after manual reduction; bleeding is a feature.
