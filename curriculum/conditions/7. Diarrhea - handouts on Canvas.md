Diarrhea

Diarrhea refers to the condition of having three or more loose or liquid bowel movements per day.
Zangfu involved in diarrhea: spleen, liver, kidney

Etiology and Pathology
Non-infective factors
Infectious factors

	Dehydration
Urinating less often than usual
Irritability
Dry mouth

Sunken anterior fontanel
Skin that isn't as elastic as usual

	General
Fever of 102 degrees Fahrenheit or higher
Abdominal pain
Blood or pus in the stool, or the stool is black, white, or red

Adult Diarrhea
Etiology and Pathology
The most common causes of diarrhea include the following:
Bacterial infections
	Viral infections
Parasites
Functional bowel disorders
	Intestinal diseases
Food intolerances and sensitivities
Reaction to medicines

Symptoms and Signs:
1, BM frequency increased significantly.
2. Stool becomes loose, sour odor, yellow color, mucus, undigested food.
3. Abdominal pain, anal heavy sensation, burning anus.

Diagnosis through stool:
Black-upper gastrointestinal bleeding
Red-lower gastrointestinal bleeding
Parasites or insects can also be directly be seen

TCM Etiology and Pathology
Affection by external pathogens: wind, summer heat, damp, cold and heat.
Dietary injury: overeating
Liver Qi stagnation overacting spleen : anger
Spleen/stomach weakness
Kidney Yang deficiency

Acute Diarrhea
Damp-cold type
Main Symptoms: Acute attach, diarrhea with watery stool, borborygmus and abdominal pain, no thirst, aversion to cold, abdominal distension, poor appetite. Fever and chill; headache, body ache, fatigue, small amount of urine.
Tongue: white and greasy coating
Pulse: slow
Treatment Principles: relieve exterior, dispel cold, transform turbidity, drain damp by diuresis
Point Prescription: LI4, Ren12, ST25, ST37, LI9, Ren8

Damp-heat type
Main Symptoms: diarrhea, urgent and sudden after abdominal pain, yellow stool with strong bad odor; burning anus, thirst and want to drink, irritability, scanty dark urine.
Tongue: red body, yellow greasy coating
Pulse: rapid slippery
Treatment Principles: clear heat, drain damp
Point Prescription: LI4, ST44, Ren12, ST25, ST36, Jin Jin Yu Ye, UB40

Food stagnation type
Main Symptoms: diarrhea after abdominal pain, refuse pressure on abdomen, pain relieved after diarrhea, stool with strong bad odor and undigested food; poor appetite, upper abdominal distention.
Tongue: thick greasy coating
Pulse: rapid slippery
Treatment Principles: remove food stagnation, promote digestion
Point Prescription: Ren12, UB20, UB21, ST36, LIV13, Ren10, Ren21, ST44

Chronic Diarrhea
Liver Qi overacting on spleen type
Main Symptoms: diarrhea after abdominal pain, pain is relieved after diarrhea; pain and diarrhea after anger or stress, borborygmus, hypochondriac pain, chest distention, belching, poor appetite.
Tongue: thin white coating, pale tongue body
Pulse: wiry
Treatment Principles: move liver qi, tonify spleen qi, stop diarrhea.
Point Prescription: UB18, UB20, Ren12, ST25, Liv3, Liv14, GB34, ST36, P6

Spleen Qi Deficiency
Main Symptoms: Loose stool after consuming oily food or eating too much, stool with undigested food, alternately watery or loose; poor appetite, upper abdominal distention after eating; sallow complexion, fatigue, weakness of limbs.
Tongue: pale body, white coating
Pulse: thready and weak
Treatment Principles: tonify spleen Qi, harmonize stomach and spleen.
Point Prescription: Ren4, Ren8, Ren9, Ren12, ST25, UB20, UB21, UB25, ST36, SP9

Kidney Yang Deficiency
Main Symptoms: Diarrhea before dawn, loose stool with undigested food in stool; diarrhea after  borborygmus, comfort after diarrhea; cold in lower abdomen and limbs, aversion to cold; impotence, sore and weak knees, low back pain. Damp and cool scrotum in male or delayed menstrual cycle with oligomenorrhea in female.
Tongue: pale body, thin white coating
Pulse: thready and weak
Treatment Principles: tonify kidneyYang
Point Prescription: UB 23, KD 3, REN 4, REN 12, UB 20, LV 13, ST 25, ST 36, DU 4, REN 8

Auricular:  Rectum, Large Intestine, Constipation, SubCortex, Sympathetic
