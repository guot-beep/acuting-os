Vomiting

Vomiting (known informally as throwing up) is the forceful expulsion of the contents of one's stomach through the mouth and sometimes the nose

TCM Etiology, Pathology and Treatment:
Invasion of external pathogens
Symptoms and Signs: Sudden vomiting, jaundice, feeling cold, aversion to wind and cold, mild fever, body ache, HA, weak extremities
Tongue: thin white coating
Pulse: superficial, tight, slightly slippery
Treatment Principles: remove pathogens, relieve exterior, regulate stomach function
Point Prescriptions: Ren 12, ST36, P6, LI4, GB20

Improper diet injuring SP/ST
Acute Condition:
Symptoms and Signs: Vomiting with undigested food, more severe after meal, comfort after vomiting; distension and fullness in the stomach.
Tongue: thick white or greasy coating
Pulse: slippery tight pulse
Treatment Principles: promote digestion, regulate stomach, relieve vomiting
Point Prescriptions: Ren10, Ren21, ST21, ST36, SP16, P6

Chronic Condition:
1. Food allergies
Symptoms and Signs: nausea, vomiting, the vomit is clear; cough with white or yellow phlegm; palpitation, dizziness, preference for sweet food, discomfort in the stomach after eating.
Tongue: white greasy moist coating
Pulse: slippery thin pulse
Treatment Principles: warm spleen yang, regulate spleen qi, resolve phlegm, regulate stomach function, and descend stomach qi
Point Prescriptions: SP4, Ren12, ST40, UB20

Emotional injury
Symptoms and Signs: vomiting acid and bitter discharges, or retching; belching; fullness and burning in chest, hypochondriac pain
Tongue: flabby, red edges, thin greasy coating
Pulse: tight, wiry and slippery
Treatment Principles: soothe liver qi, regulate stomach
Point Prescriptions: LI4, Liv3, Ren13, ST21, P6, SP4

Deficiency of SP/ST
Spleen Yang Xu:
Symptoms and Signs: repeated vomiting, nausea and vomiting after tired or improper diet, clear discharges; pale complexion, preference for warmth, aversion to cold; pyloric obstruction, GI bleeding.
Tongue: pale body, thin white coating
Pulse: thin, weak
Treatment Principles: warm middle Jiao, Tonify spleen, regulate stomach function.
Point Prescription: Ren12, P6, ST36, UB20, UB21, Liv13, Ren4, ST25

Stomach Yin Xu:
Symptoms and Signs: retching, dry mouth, dry throat.
Tongue: red body, very thin or peeled coating
Pulse: thin, rapid
Treatment principle: Tonify stomach yin, relieve vomiting
Point Prescription: Ren12, Ren6, Ren3, ST36, P6, KD3, SP4, UB20, UB21

Other Treatment Options:
Auricular: ST, Liver, Sympathetic, Subcortex, Shenmen

Cautions:
Aspiration of vomit-Vomiting can be dangerous if the gastric content enters the respiratory tract.
