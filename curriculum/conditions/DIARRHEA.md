                                        Infantile  Diarrhea

Infantile diarrhea is characterized by disharmony of spleen and stomach with frequent bowel movements, and loose or watery feces.
It may occur in any season, but more frequently in summer and fall.

Etiology and Pathogenesis

Irregular diet
Contaminated food and milk intake                 Sp & St xu
Improper attending                                         unable to digest & absorption

Differentiation

Abdominal distension accompanied by borborygmi and abdominal pain.
The pain usually followed by bowel movement, and relieved after bowel movement.
There are several times of BM a day with sour and putrid feces.
