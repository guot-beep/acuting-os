Lid  Abscess

Lid abscess (stye ), also named meibomianitis because it is an acute inflammation of sebaceous glands at the edge of the eyelids.   In TCM it is also known as " needle eye " and " wheat abscess " because of its shape and appearance.

Etiology and Pathogenesis

A sty occurs when bacteria infect one of the tiny glands at the base of the eyelids hairs.  The gland then becomes inflamed.

Differantiation

At first, the symptoms are only local redness and swelling in the area of the stye.  Often a tiny and hard pimple is present at the affected  lid.  The infection usually is painful and tender only if it is touched.  Then very soon the infection gets worse, redder and bigger, very tender with pressure, the pain aggravated with head down.   About 3 - 4 days after the onset, a yellow purulent dot appears at the tip of the pimple, then the infection bursts and drains (out the pus flows).  Styes do not affect vision unless the swelling blocks vision.

Western Treatment

Put hot compresses on the eyes 3 to 4 times a day for several days.  The compresses help the body fight the infection and may speed up drainage of the stye.  Antibiotic drops may also be applied.
Sometimes a stye needs to be opened and drained for quick healing.
