Wind Stroke

Wind Stroke, or Zhong Feng, a TCM term meaning “Catching Wind”, is known as Cerebrovascular Accident (CVA) which the arteries in the brain become blocked or rupture and results in hemiplegia.

TCM Etiology & Pathology
1. Liver Yang excess and kidney yin deficiency
2. Phlegm and dampness accumulation
3. Over strain and stress
4. Lifestyle factors

Mechanisms of disease:
There are five very important factors here:
1. Wind
2. Fire
3. Phlegm
4. Stasis
5. Deficiency
a. Blood
b. Yin
c. Body fluid

Manifestations
Stroke refers to 2 types
Meridian stroke
There are 3 conditions:
Wind invasion obstruction: aversion to cold, fever. T:thin coating, P: floating
Liver & kidney Yin Xu, stirring up wind: sore low back, tinnitus. T: red, P: thin
Phlegm heat, wind phlegm up disturbing: profuse phlegm, constipation. T: greasy, P: slippery

Zang-Fu stroke

Excess type (Close type):
Yang Bi condition
Main Symptoms: tight jaw, closed mouth, clenched fists, tight limbs, eyes opened, rough breathing.  Red face, hot body
Tongue: red body, dark dry yellow grease coating
Pulse: slippery, wiry, rapid
Treatment Principles: sedate liver Yang, remove inner wind, descend Qi and blood, and open orifices
Point Prescription:  Du20, Du26, Ht1, P8, KD1, GB20, Liv2, Liv3, SJ5, Du15, Du16

Yin Bi condition
Main Symptoms: sudden falling down with convulsion, pale and tight face, dark libs, clenched teeth, jaw and mouth, eyes closed, rattling sound of phlegm in throat or rough, fast breath, or wheezing, no bowel movement, no sweat, cold limbs, cooler body temperature
Tongue: pale and dimmer body, thick, greasy coating
Pulse: slow, deep and slippery
Treatment Principles: Remove turbid phlegm and inner wind, open orifices of internal organs
Point Prescription: Du16, Du20, Du26, Ht1, P8, KD1, Ren12, Ren17, ST40, SP9, P6, GB20, GB34, 4 gates

Deficiency type (Open type):
Yang Tuo condition
Main Symptoms: sudden falling, open mouth with eyes closed, pale face, incontinence of body, cold limbs.
Tongue: Pale, flabby with teeth marks, moist coating
Pulse: slow, deep, weak thready
Treatment Principles: tonify yang
Point Prescription: Ren4, Ren6, Du20, Du4, UB35, Ren8

Yin Tuo condition
Main Symptoms: suddenly fall down with loss of consciousness or semiconscious; profuse sticky warm sweating, red cheeks, warm limbs, incontinence of urine/stools, rapid and rough breath.
Tongue: scarlet red, thin body. Peeled coating
Pulse: thready, weak, racing
Treatment Principles: Tonify Yin

Convalescence Stage Treatment
There are 3 patterns for the convalescence stage.
Liver and kidney Yin Deficiency
Main Symptoms: Headache, dizziness, semiconsciousness and then loss of consciousness. Twitching of muscles and skin; red face, trembling or stiff tongue; hemiplegia, flaccidity, weakness of lower extremities; tinnitus, dysphagia, aphasia; low back pain; 5 center heat, low grade fever, constipation.
Tongue: scarlet, small and thin; peeled with cracks
Pulse: deep, wiry, thin, rapid
Treatment Principles: nourish liver and kidney yin, expel inner wind and prevent liver yang rising
Point Prescription: Kid1, Kid3, Kid6, SP6, Liv3, Liv8, GB39, Du15, Du16, UB23, UB52, UB43

Damp phlegm accumulation
Main Symptoms: semiconsciousness, heavy sensation of body; pale face, chest distension, nausea, vomiting with mucous, poor appetite, snoring with rattling sound of phlegm in throat, loose stool.
Tongue: pale, flabby with teeth marks; thick and greasy coating
Pulse: slippery, weak
Treatment Principles: Tonify spleen qi, drain damp phlegm and inner wind
Point Prescription: GB8, GB20, Yintang, Ren17, Ren22, ST36, SP6, UB20, UB21; SP9, Ren12, Ren17, ST40; GB20, DU16, GB34

Qi and Blood Deficiency
Main Symptoms: Exhaustion, pale face, poor spirit; poor memory, forgetfulness, bad concentration; insomnia, nightmares; numbness or flaccidity of one side, local weakness; twitching of muscles, skin and eyelids; hemiplegia, aphasia; palpitations, poor appetite, loose stool or diarrhea, alopecia
Tongue: pale with thin white coating
Pulse: thin and weak, deep, thin, thready
Treatment Principles: tonify qi and nourish blood
Point Prescription: Si Shen Cong, ST36, SP6, UB15, UB17, UB20, UB21, SP10, Ren14, Ren15, GB20, GB34, Du16

Sequelae Stage Treatment
Central Facial Paralysis
Main Symptoms: Deviation of tongue and mouth. Drinking water and eating will fall out of the corner of the mouth on the affected side. Lateral nasolabial groove disappears on one side. If severe, cannot close eye on affected side. Mouth deviates to the healthy side. Numbness limbs.。
Tongue: pale body with white greasy coating
Pulse: wiry and slippery
Treatment Principles: expel wind, dissolve phlegm, open meridian
Point Prescription: Yintang, Taiyang, Qianzheng, LI20, SI18, SI19, ST3, ST4, ST6, ST7, Ren24
