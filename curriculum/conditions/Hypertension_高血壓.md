## Definition 定義

> **Hypertension** is defined as resting arterial **systolic BP > 140 mmHg** and/or **diastolic BP > 90 mmHg**.
> **高血壓**定義為靜息狀態下動脈**收縮壓 > 140 mmHg** 且/或**舒張壓 > 90 mmHg**。

- Affects ~640 million worldwide / 約6.4億人受影響
- ~50 million Americans / 約5000萬美國人

---

| Type / 類型 | Description / 描述 |
|---|---|
| **Primary Hypertension 原發性高血壓** | No known cause / 原因不明 |
| **Secondary Hypertension 繼發性高血壓** | Due to underlying condition / 由其他疾病引起 |

## Western Medicine 西醫

### Etiology & Pathology 病因病理

#### Primary Hypertension 原發性高血壓

No exact cause known. Contributing factors / 沒有確切原因，以下為可能因素：

| Lifestyle Factors / 生活因素 | Medical/Other Factors / 醫療/其他因素 |
|---|---|
| Smoking 抽菸 | Older age 年齡增長 |
| Lack of physical activity 缺乏運動 | Family history 家族史 |
| Too much salt 高鹽飲食 | Environmental factors 環境因素 |
| Too many fatty foods 高脂飲食 | Chronic kidney disease 慢性腎病 |
| Too much alcohol 過量飲酒 | Adrenal & thyroid disorders 腎上腺與甲狀腺疾病 |
| Stress 壓力 | Loneliness 孤獨 |

#### Secondary Hypertension 繼發性高血壓

- Kidney disease 腎臟疾病
- Adrenal glands 腎上腺
- Pregnancy 懷孕
- Medications 藥物

---

### Hypertensive Crisis 高血壓危象

> Severely elevated BP that confers high risk of complications.
> 血壓急劇升高，併發症風險極高。

**Manifestations 症狀：**
- Headaches 頭痛
- Dizziness 頭暈
- Vertigo 眩暈
- Tinnitus 耳鳴
- Fainting episodes 昏厥
- Anxiety 焦慮

---

### When to Refer to ER 轉介急診指徵

- Systolic > **180** and/or Diastolic > **120**
- Blurry vision / vision disturbances 視力模糊或視覺障礙
- Headache 頭痛
- Dizziness 頭暈
- Nausea or appetite changes 噁心或食慾改變
- Hypertensive crisis 高血壓危象

## Prognosis 預後

> With improper treatment, hypertension can cause **organ failure** of the heart, cerebrum, and kidney.
> 若治療不當，高血壓可導致**心臟、大腦及腎臟衰竭**。
