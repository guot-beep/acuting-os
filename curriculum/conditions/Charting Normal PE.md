Charting Example of a Normal Physical Examination

Vitals:	H:	W:	BP:	P:	RR:	T:
Impression (AKA General Appearance):
A ____ year old _____ (gender) patient NAD (no apparent distress) dressed appropriately for place and season with appropriate affect and communication intact.
Head:
Hair intact of a _____color, no areas of alopecia, scalp without redness, inflammation, nodules or lesions; no tenderness to palpation.
Eye:
Inspection of conjunctivae and lids: conjunctivae without redness; injection or discharge; lids without swelling, redness or lesions; eyelashes intact. Examination of pupils and irises (e.g. reaction to light and accommodation, size, symmetry): PERLAA, equal bilaterally. Ophthalmoscopic examination of optic discs: Size and symmetry intact, A/V ratio intact, no exudates, hemorrhages, discs intact, macula.
Ear:
Inspection reveals no lesions, exudate or scarring of external structure. Otoscopic exam of ears reveals no redness or exudate in external canal; Tympanic membrane intact without injection, redness, exudate or scarring of TM; COL (Cone of light) intact; Gross hearing test by finger rub intact bilaterally.
Nose:
Inspection of external structures reveals no lesions, exudate or scarring of external structure; Mucosa, septum and turbinates intact without swelling, redness or lesions noted.
Mouth:
Inspection of lips, teeth and gums revealed no lesions, scarring of external structures, lips mobile and symmetrical.  Teeth intact without caries, gums without redness, swelling or lesions; buccal mucosa without scarring, lesions or redness; tongue without lesions, redness; tonsils with swelling, redness, or 	lesions; posterior oropharynx without redness, lesions or exudate; hard and soft palate without injection, lesions, redness or swelling; salivary glands without lesions or exudate.
Neck:
Structures symmetrical, trachea midline; thyroid palpable without swelling, nodules or tenderness; superficial, deep and posterior cervical, supra- and infraclavicular lymph nodes non-palpable; submandibular glands palpable without tenderness.
Lungs:
Clear to anterior, posterior and lateral auscultation without adventitious sounds or rubs, no wheezes; Percussion without hyper-resonance, dullness; no respiratory effort noted; no tactile fremitus; digits without clubbing or cyanosis.
Cardiovascular:
RRR without extra sounds, murmurs, clicks or gallops; no heaves or thrills; carotid arteries without bruits; abdominal aorta without enlargement or bruits; femoral pulses without bruits; pedal pulses intact; no varicosities or pre-tibial edema; digits without clubbing or cyanosis.
Breasts:
Symmetrical and move freely. No tenderness, masses, lumps or lesions and without nipple discharge.
Abdomen:
Symmetrical without external scars or lesions; auscultation reveals bowel sounds X 4 quadrants; no tenderness or masses on light or deep palpation; bimanual reveals no hepatosplenomegaly or tenderness; no hernia 	palpated.
Anorectal:
External exam reveals no lesions, redness or swelling; sphincter tone intact; digital exam reveals no hemorrhoids or masses; stool obtained/not obtained for occult blood test.
Male Genitourinary:
External exam notes circumcised/non-circumcised penis without external scars, lesions, redness and with hair growth appropriate to age; scrotum reveal no tenderness, mass, hydrocele or spermatocele; Prostate: digitorectal exam performed in the _________ position revealed symmetrical prostate without enlargement or nodules.
Female Genitourinary:
External genitalia and urethra intact without redness, swelling, discharge or lesions; hair growth appropriate to age; vaginal tone intact without cystocele or rectocele; BSU (Bartholin's, Skein's, Peri-urethral glands) 	without enlargement, tenderness, or discharge; rugae intact without redness, discharge or lesions; cervix 	parous/non-parous without lesions, 	discharge or redness located midline/right/left and facing 	midline/anterior/posterior/superior/inferior; 	samples obtained from ectocervix/endocervix using 	broom/brush/swab for pap smear/HPV testing; bimanual exam revealed no cervical motion 	tenderness; uterus without lumps or enlargement measuring @ 5 X 7 X 2 cm; ovaries palpated without tenderness  or nodules @ 2 X 1.5 X 2 cm bilaterally.
Lymphatic:
Palpate lymph nodes in two or more areas, noting area of neck, axillae, groin, or other area and whether nodes are palpable, size and tenderness.
Musculoskeletal:
Gait and station intact; _________ joints palpated without tenderness, swelling or heat; no lesions palpated; No misalignment, asymmetry, crepitus, tenderness, masses, or effusion palpated; active ROM intact to ______degrees without crepitus, contracture or pain; passive and resisted ROM revealed no dislocation, subluxation or laxity; muscle strength and tone intact without atrophy; no abnormal movement noted.
Skin:
Inspection reveals skin texture, moisture and turgor intact and with no abnormal lesions, rashes or ulceration of skin or subcutaneous tissue; no indurations or tightening noted; note location, size and color of all scars; note degree of freckling present; note any moles or other lesions estimating size, color and whether borders are discrete; note any redness, discoloration or abnormal structure of any nail; note any petechiae or bruising present.
Neuro:
CN (Cranial Nerves) I-XII intact; ______ DTRs (deep tendon reflexes) intact +2; sensation intact to light touch, sharp, vibration, proprioception throughout; Babinski negative; Romberg, Pronator Drift, Rapid 	Alternating Movements, Heel to Shin, Balance intact.
Psych:
Description of patient judgement and insight (may be included in generals unless performing full exam); brief assessment of mental status including orientation to place, person and time; recent and remote memory; mood and affect including depression, agitation, anxiety or altered response (if mood and affect are altered, include an assessment tool to evaluate depth of alteration).
