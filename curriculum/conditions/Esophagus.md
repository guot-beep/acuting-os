## Overview 概述

The esophagus is a muscular tube connecting the pharynx to the stomach, lined by **stratified squamous epithelium**, functioning via coordinated peristalsis.

食道是連接咽部與胃部的肌肉管道，由**複層鱗狀上皮**覆蓋，透過蠕動將食物送入胃中。

> 🔑 **The 4 most important esophageal diseases for NCCAOM:**
> - GERD 胃食道逆流
> - Barrett Esophagus 巴瑞特食道
> - Esophageal Adenocarcinoma 食道腺癌
> - Esophageal SCC 食道鱗狀細胞癌

## Disease Pathways 病理路徑

### GERD Pathway ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
GERD (LES dysfunction)
        ↓
Reflux Esophagitis 逆流性食道炎
        ↓
Barrett Esophagus — Intestinal Metaplasia 腸化生
        ↓
Dysplasia 異型增生
        ↓
Esophageal Adenocarcinoma 食道腺癌 (distal 遠端)
```

### SCC Pathway ⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
Smoking + Alcohol + Achalasia
        ↓
Squamous Dysplasia 鱗狀上皮異型增生
        ↓
Squamous Cell Carcinoma (upper/middle 上/中段)
```

## Clinical Manifestations 臨床表現

| Symptom | Chinese | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Heartburn | 胃灼熱 | Classic GERD |
| Regurgitation | 胃酸逆流 | Classic GERD |
| Dysphagia 🚩 | 吞嚥困難 | Red flag — rule out malignancy |
| Odynophagia | 吞嚥疼痛 | Severe esophagitis or cancer |
| Weight loss 🚩 | 體重減輕 | Red flag — cancer until proven otherwise |
