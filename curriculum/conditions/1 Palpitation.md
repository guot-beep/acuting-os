Palpitation

Palpitation is a term that the patient describes the feeling he/she can feel
an abnormality in the normal beating of the heart
an abnormality of heartbeat that ranges from often unnoticed skipped beats or accelerated heart rate to very noticeable changes

Manifestations:
Rapid heartbeats
Skipped heartbeats sensation
Pounding feeling
Chest fullness
Syncope
Heart palpitations can occur whether you're active or at rest
Duration can be from a few seconds to several hours

TCM Etiology & Pathology
Excessive Condition:
Mental disorder
Phlegm damp accumulation
Pathogenic factors invading heart
Chronic disease

Deficiency Condition:
Overstrain
Chronic disease
