LIN SYNDROME

Lín Zhèng,  a syndrome characterized by discomfort during urination with symptoms of urgency, increased frequency, oliguria, and painful, difficult urination.

Etiology and Pathology
In Western Medicine
Lin syndrome includes the Western conditions of urinary renal calculi, milky urine, Neoplasms of the bladder, prostate, tuberculosis of kidney, and urethra, urinary retention, prostatitis and UTI

1. Diet: internal damp heat from spleen damp
2. Environment: Exogenous Damp-Heat invades the Urinary Bladder

4. Damp-Heat disturbs the Urinary Bladder’s ability
5. Old age or chronic illness: causes kidney Qi Xu

Differentiations:
Lin Syndrome and Urinary Retention
The similar: scanty/difficult urination
The difference:
Lin Syndrome-frequent/painful urination, normal urine amount/day
Urinary Retention-no pain urination, less normal urine amount/day or no urine at all.
 
Blood Lin and hematuria
The similar: blood in urine
The difference:
Blood Lin- dripping/painful urination
Hematuria - no pain or very mild pain or burning sensation
 
Cream Lin and Milky Urine
The similar: cloudy urine
The difference:
Cream Lin-pain
Milky Urine-no pain
