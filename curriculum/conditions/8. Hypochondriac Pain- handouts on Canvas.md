Hypochondriac Pain

Hypochondriac Pain refers to the condition of a disorder characterized by pain over the unilateral or bilateral sides of the hypochondriac region.

Zangfu involved in diarrhea: Liver and GB, spleen and stomach, kidney

Western Etiology and Pathology
Liver cirrhosis, biliary tract infections, cholecystitis, hepatitis, intercostals neuralgia

TCM Etiology and Pathology
Excessive Condition:
Liver Qi Stagnation
Blood Stasis: Failure in free flow of qi and blood
Dampness Heat in liver and gall bladder: phlegm damp invasion, overeating

Deficiency Condition:
Liver Yin Xu
Fluid Retention in Hypochondium
Shao Yang Meridian Disease: Stagnation of pathogens

	Deficiency Condition

Pain at the left hypochondrium: food stagnation, liver qi stagnation.

Pain at the right hypochondrium: food stagnation, phlegm fluid retention.

Symptoms and Signs:
Excessive Conditions
Liver Qi stagnation type
Main Symptoms: hypochondriac pain as distention or stabbing and wondering pain, pain may radiates to the upper back and shoulder; belching, poor appetite, relieved or exacerbated due to emotional fluctuations.
Tongue: thin white coating, pale tongue body
Pulse: wiry
Treatment Principles: move liver qi
Point Prescription: LI4, Liv3, GB34, SJ5, SP6, SJ8, Ren16, UB18, Liv14, Liv13, UB21, GB34, HT7

Blood-stasis
Main Symptoms: Stabbing pain in hypochondriac regions, it is fixed and more severe at night, refuse pressure, may find mass at the regions.
Tongue: dark purple body
Pulse: deep and choppy
Treatment Principles: move blood, open meridian
Point Prescription: Sp21, GB24, Liv2, UB17, P4, LI15, SJ14,

Damp-heat type
Main Symptoms: hypochondriac pain at the right side only, constant or periodically sharp pain, pain may radiates to the upper back and shoulder, refuses pressure; fever, nausea, vomiting, chest distention, poor appetite especially fatty food, dry mouth and bitter taste in mouth; jaundice on eyes and skin; yellowish scanty and difficulty urine.
Tongue: red body, yellow greasy coating
Pulse: rapid slippery
Treatment Principles: clear heat, drain damp
Point Prescription: Liv14, GB24, SJ6, GB34, Liv3, Ren12, ST36, LI4, LI11, SJ5, Du14,

Deficiency Conditions
Liver Yin Xu type
Main Symptoms: hypochondriac dull and constant pain, worse when fatigue; dry mouth and throat, dry eyes, irritability, dizziness, blurry eyes.
Tongue: red body, peered coating
Pulse: wiry, thin and rapid
Treatment Principles: nourish yin, sooth liver, regulate qi, open channel
Point Prescription: Ht6, UB15, UB18, UB23, UB43, SP10, SP6,

Avoid strong stimulation on emotion
Avoid fatty food
Avoid alcohol
