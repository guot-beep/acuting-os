                                             Stiff  Neck ( Torticollis )

Stiff neck here refers to wryneck caused by an inappropriate sleeping posture or attack of wind cold on the neck/nape that leads to disturbance of local circulation of Qi in involved meridians.  It occurs mostly in adults. In TCM it is called as luo zhen??.

Its main manifestation are stiffness and pain of the neck and nape on one side, and wry neck towards one side with motor impairment, most patients keep their heads to the affected side in order to reduce pain and spasm.  The possible manifestation might be as follows:

Stiffness and pain may spread to the shoulder and arm on the affected side, occasionally both sides of the nape and shoulder may be involved.
Palpation shows tenderness and muscular spasm in the neck/nape area, but no redness, heat and swelling.
Wind-cold sx may be present along with neck/nape stiffness and pain.
If the pain and stiffness is longer than a week even with treatment, patients should be sent to MD to evaluate for possible cervical vertebrae disorders.

Treatment

1.  Principle
     Expel wind and disperse cold, and relax tendons, activate blood and Qi circulation,
     Warm the meridians, remove stagnation.
     Local points, plus points from SI, UB, SJ and GB.
     Reducing techniques and moxibustion.

2.  Prescription
     Du 14,  UB 10,  SI 3,  14,  GB 39,  GB 20,  Bailao (2cun above Du 14, 1cun lateral),  Ashi points
                                                                                     (or/and 1 cun below hairline, 1cun lateral to Du)
     Secondary Points:
     Wind-cold:  LI 4,  Lu 7,  SJ 5
     Pain spreading to the scapular:  SI 12,  SI 15
     Cervical spondylosis:  Du 15,  HTJJ at the affected vertebrae level
     Luozhen
     Inability of flexion and extension :  UB 60,  Lu 7
     Difficult in rotating neck:  SI 7
     Inducing Qi technique ??:  SJ 10  For patients withlocal hypertenderness

3.  Apply inducing Qi technique on Luozhen.
     Direct patient keep moving neck at the same time while needling.
     If the neck pain remains, switch to SJ 10 with inducing Qi technique.

Other Therapies

1.  Cupping:  UB 11, UB 12
2.  Moxibustion:  Ashi points
3.  Electroacupuncture:  for severe cases
4.  If unabated by needling Luozhen,  needle Ashi points, or SI 3, or GB 39.
5.  Tuina
6.  Seven Star Needle:  HTJJ at C level
7.  Auricular acupuncture
