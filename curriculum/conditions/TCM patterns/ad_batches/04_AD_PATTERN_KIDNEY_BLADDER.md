# AD Pattern Enrichment 04 — Kidney / Bladder 腎與膀胱

## 腎陰虛 · Kidney Yin Deficiency

- **Canonical ID:** `pattern.kidney_yin_deficiency` **only if this exact ID already exists in `pattern_registry.json`; otherwise MATCH/STAGE, do not create it automatically.**
- **AD role:** secondary clinical enrichment source

### Clinical Manifestations 臨床表現
- 眩暈、耳鳴或聽力減退 — Dizziness, tinnitus/hearing decline
- 健忘 — Poor memory
- 腰痠腰痛或無力 — Low-back soreness/weakness
- 骨、膝、足跟痠痛 — Bone/knee/heel aching
- 夜汗 — Night sweats
- 五心煩熱、午後低熱、顴紅 — Five-center heat, afternoon low fever, malar flush
- 口咽乾燥、口渴 — Dry mouth/throat and thirst
- 便乾或便秘 — Dry stool/constipation
- 生殖系表現可見遺精/早洩等 — Reproductive deficiency manifestations may occur

### Tongue / Coat 舌象 / 苔
- **Tongue 舌:** 紅 — Red
- **Coating 苔:** 少苔或無苔 — Little or none

### Pulse 脈象
- **Pulse 脈:** 細數 — Thready and rapid

### Treatment Principle 治則
- 滋補腎陰；虛火明顯時兼清虛熱 / Nourish Kidney Yin; subdue deficiency fire when present

## 腎陽虛 · Kidney Yang Deficiency

- **Canonical ID:** `pattern.kidney_yang_deficiency` **only if this exact ID already exists in `pattern_registry.json`; otherwise MATCH/STAGE, do not create it automatically.**
- **AD role:** secondary clinical enrichment source

### Clinical Manifestations 臨床表現
- 腰部冷、痠、弱、痛 — Low back cold/weak/aching
- 畏寒、四肢冷 — Chills/aversion to cold/cold limbs
- 精神疲憊、面色淡白 — Debility/fatigue/pale face
- 膝冷痛 — Cold aching knees
- 小便清長頻數或夜尿；亦可尿少水腫 — Frequent clear urine/nocturia or oliguria with edema
- 眩暈耳鳴 — Dizziness/tinnitus
- 性慾低下、陽痿等生殖功能減退 — Reduced sexual function may occur
- 可伴便溏或五更瀉 — Loose stool/early-morning diarrhea may occur

### Tongue / Coat 舌象 / 苔
- **Tongue 舌:** 淡、胖或腫 — Pale and swollen
- **Coating 苔:** 薄白 — Thin white

### Pulse 脈象
- **Pulse 脈:** 沉細，或沉弱，可遲 — Deep-thready/deep-weak, possibly slow

### Treatment Principle 治則
- 溫補腎陽 / Warm and reinforce Kidney Yang

## 腎精不足 · Kidney Jing Deficiency

- **AD role:** secondary clinical enrichment source

### Clinical Manifestations 臨床表現
- 眩暈、耳鳴、聽力逐漸下降 — Dizziness, tinnitus, gradual hearing decline
- 腰膝痠軟 — Weak/aching low back and knees
- 健忘 — Poor memory
- 髮早白或脫髮 — Premature graying/hair loss
- 性與生殖功能減退 — Reduced sexual/reproductive function
- 骨脆或步行無力 — Brittle bones / weak lower limbs in adult contexts

- **Tongue 舌:** 依偏陰/偏陽而變；AD 常見淡舌情境 — Varies with Yin/Yang subtype; AD often shows pale tongue in contexts
- **Coating 苔:** 常見薄白但非固定 — Often thin white, not universal

### Pulse 脈象
- **Pulse 脈:** 細弱或沉細，依陰陽偏頗而變 — Thready-weak/deep-thready depending subtype

### Treatment Principle 治則
- 補腎填精 / Reinforce Kidneys and benefit Jing

- **AD role:** secondary clinical enrichment source

### Clinical Manifestations 臨床表現
- 尿頻、尿急 — Urinary frequency/urgency
- 尿痛、灼熱或排尿滴瀝 — Painful/burning urination or dribbling
- 尿流突然中斷情境 — Interrupted urinary stream in some contexts
- 尿濁，可見膿血或砂石 — Turbid urine; pus/blood/sand/stones in some contexts
- 少腹脹滿不適 — Suprapubic fullness/discomfort
- 可伴腰痛、發熱、口渴口乾 — Low-back pain, fever, thirst/dry mouth may accompany
- 結石情境可見絞痛向腹股溝放射 — Colicky radiating pain in stone context

- **Tongue 舌:** 紅 — Red
- **Coating 苔:** 黃 — Yellow

### Pulse 脈象
- **Pulse 脈:** 滑數 — Slippery and rapid

### Treatment Principle 治則
- 清熱利濕、通淋 / Clear Heat, drain Dampness, promote urination
