# 05 — AD Special Differentiation Systems Supplement

The first AD enrichment pack focused mostly on common Zang-Fu / Qi-Blood-Fluid / pathogen patterns.

A broader review of American Dragon confirms that AD also contains substantial material for classic special differentiation systems.

These patterns should be used **only if they already exist in the current canonical registry** or are deliberately staged for future review.

---

# A. Six-Channel Differentiation 六經辨證

## Tai Yang examples

AD contains blocks such as:

- Tai Yang Zhong Feng
- Tai Yang Shang Han
- Tai Yang Biao Fu
- Tai Yang–Yang Ming
- Tai Yang–Tai Yin
- Tai Yang–Shao Yin combinations

Representative URLs:

- https://www.americandragon.com/conditions/Arthralgia.html
- https://www.americandragon.com/conditions/Pyelonephritis.html
- https://www.americandragon.com/conditions/Gangrene.html
- https://www.americandragon.com/conditions/Dizziness.html

### Tai Yang Zhong Feng core cues from AD

- aversion to Wind
- fever
- spontaneous sweating
- headache
- nasal symptoms
- thin white coating
- floating/moderate pulse

Treatment orientation:
- relieve Exterior
- expel Wind
- harmonize Ying and Wei

### Tai Yang Shang Han core cues from AD

- severe chills
- milder fever
- no sweat
- head/body aches
- stiff neck/shoulders
- thin white coating
- floating tight pulse

Treatment orientation:
- release Exterior
- expel Cold
- diffuse Lung Qi

- Tai Yang–Yang Ming combinations

Representative URLs:

## Shao Yang

AD contains Shao Yang Jing and mixed Shao Yang–Yang Ming patterns.

Representative source:

- https://www.americandragon.com/conditions/Stress.html

Core Shao Yang cues often include:
- alternating chills and fever
- hypochondriac discomfort
- nausea / reduced appetite
- irritability/restlessness
- wiry pulse in many contexts

---

## Tai Yin

AD contains:

- Tai Yin deficiency
- Tai Yin accumulation of Cold-Damp
- Tai Yin combinations

Representative source:

- https://www.americandragon.com/conditions/Anorxia.html

Cold-Damp example:
- fatigue
- aversion to cold
- diarrhea/loose stools
- middle-jiao fullness
- no thirst
- pale tongue
- greasy white coat
- deep weak slow pulse

## Shao Yin

Do not merge these with generic Kidney Yang/Yin deficiency without explicit canonical decision.

Representative discovery:

These require careful canonical matching.

# B. Wei-Qi-Ying-Xue 衛氣營血

American Dragon contains explicit Warm-Disease stage labels.

## Wei Stage

Examples include:

- Wei Stage Damp-Heat
- Summerheat with Qi/Yin deficiency in a Wei-stage context
- Wind-Heat variants

Representative source:
- https://www.americandragon.com/conditions/Weakness.html

## Qi Stage

Examples include:

- Qi Stage Damp-Heat
- Qi Stage Gallbladder Heat
- Qi Stage lingering heat
- Spleen/Stomach Damp-Heat

AD formula-syndrome data also contains Qi-stage labels.

## Ying Stage

Representative source:

- https://www.americandragon.com/conditions/Anemia.html
- https://www.americandragon.com/conditions/LupusErythematosus.html

Typical AD Ying Stage Heat features:
- fever worse at night
- insomnia / irritability
- mild thirst without strong desire to drink
- mental/speech disturbance in more severe contexts
- deep-red tongue
- little/no coating
- thready rapid pulse

## Xue Stage

Representative sources:

- https://www.americandragon.com/conditions/Epistaxis.html
- https://www.americandragon.com/conditions/Bronchiectasis.html
- https://www.americandragon.com/conditions/RheumaticFever.html

AD distinguishes:
- Xue Stage Heat
- excess heat causing bleeding
- Blood Heat generating Wind
- Xue-stage Yin collapse patterns

These are NOT the same as the generic `blood_heat` mechanism Pattern.

# D. Channel / Bi / Wind-Phlegm

AD formula data contains concepts such as:

- Wind-Cold-Damp Bi
- Wind-Damp-Heat Bi
- channel obstruction by Wind-Cold-Damp + Blood Stasis + Phlegm
- Wind-Phlegm obstructing head/face channels
- Phlegm-Heat
- Phlegm obstructing Heart orifices

These may cross:
- pathogenic-factor classification
- channel/Bi classification
- Zang-Fu Pattern classification

Use the current canonical Pattern concept as the deciding boundary.

---

# E. Important rule

Do not try to make the canonical Pattern registry equal to every granular phrase American Dragon uses.

The canonical registry should contain concepts useful for:

- Board study
- TCM differentiation
- clinical case linking
- stable many-to-many knowledge graph relations

Granular source phrases can remain aliases, evidence, or staging candidates.
