# Acupoint Source Staging

This directory is a review-only intake lane for filling gaps in the 361-point
knowledge layer.

Rules:

- Never overwrite `data/acupoints/361.json` from this directory.
- Preserve exact point codes and source locators.
- Fill empty fields only after a conflict preview.
- Keep imported material `draft` until field-level review.
- WHO and GB/T location standards do not authorize inferred needling, moxa,
  efficacy, or treatment claims.
- General safety guidance is not a point-specific contraindication.
- Do not commit complete copyrighted books or PDFs; retain URLs, page locators,
  and short structured factual extractions only.
