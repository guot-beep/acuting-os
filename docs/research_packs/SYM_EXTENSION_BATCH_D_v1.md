CR-008. 20 additional symptoms/findings. Under the current rule, each has a recognized biomedical descriptive term and therefore classifies as `both`.

## `sym.epigastric_pain` — Epigastric pain / 胃脘痛
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: epigastric pain
- body_region: `digestive`
- red_flags: Severe sudden pain, GI bleeding, syncope, rigid abdomen, chest-pressure features or persistent vomiting are urgent.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000593.htm
