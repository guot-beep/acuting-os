# SYM TCM-HEAVY EXTENSION BATCH C v1

**RESEARCH STAGING / NOT CANONICAL**

CR-004. 24 TCM-familiar findings that also have biomedical descriptive terms. Under the approved rule they are therefore `both`, not TCM-only.

## `sym.spontaneous_sweating` — Spontaneous/excessive sweating / 自汗
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: excessive sweating / hyperhidrosis
- body_region: `systemic`
- red_flag_note: Sweating at rest or without expected heat/exertion has biomedical correlates; evaluate secondary causes if new/persistent.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/sweat.html
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/007259.htm

## `sym.night_sweats` — Night sweats / 盜汗
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: night sweats
- body_region: `systemic`
- red_flag_note: Persistent drenching night sweats with fever, cough, weight loss, lymphadenopathy or other systemic symptoms need medical evaluation; active TB can include night sweats.
- sources:
  - https://www.cdc.gov/tb/signs-symptoms/index.html

## `sym.bitter_taste` — Bitter taste / 口苦
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: dysgeusia / altered taste
- body_region: `ears_nose_throat`
- red_flag_note: Persistent new taste disturbance is a biomedical symptom; review medications, oral/dental disease, smell disorders and neurologic/systemic context.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003050.htm

## `sym.dry_mouth` — Dry mouth / 口乾
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: xerostomia
- body_region: `ears_nose_throat`
- red_flag_note: Persistent xerostomia can reflect medication effects, Sjögren syndrome, diabetes, salivary disease or treatment effects; dysphagia/dehydration/infection increase concern.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/drymouth.html

## `sym.excessive_thirst` — Excessive thirst / 口渴
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: polydipsia / excessive thirst
- body_region: `systemic`
- red_flag_note: Marked persistent thirst with polyuria, dehydration, altered mental status, vomiting or metabolic symptoms warrants medical evaluation.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/drymouth.html

## `sym.aversion_to_cold` — Cold intolerance / aversion to cold / 畏寒
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: cold intolerance
- body_region: `systemic`
- red_flag_note: Persistent cold intolerance may occur with anemia, hypothyroidism, vascular disease or chronic illness; sudden systemic deterioration requires broader evaluation.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003095.htm

## `sym.chills` — Chills / 惡寒 / 寒戰
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: chills / shivering
- body_region: `systemic`
- red_flag_note: Chills with high fever, confusion, stiff neck, dyspnea, abdominal pain or urinary burning/frequency require prompt assessment.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003091.htm

## `sym.heat_intolerance` — Heat intolerance / 畏熱
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: heat intolerance
- body_region: `systemic`
- red_flag_note: Acute overheating with confusion, collapse or very high body temperature is an emergency; chronic heat intolerance can have endocrine/medication causes.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003094.htm

## `sym.cold_extremities` — Cold extremities / 四肢冰冷
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: cold hands/feet / peripheral coldness
- body_region: `musculoskeletal`
- red_flag_note: New unilateral cold/pale/painful limb, absent pulse, severe numbness or weakness can indicate vascular or neurologic emergency.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003095.htm

## `sym.urinary_frequency` — Urinary frequency / 小便頻數
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: urinary frequency
- body_region: `urogenital`
- red_flag_note: Frequency with fever, flank pain, dysuria, hematuria, pregnancy, severe thirst or systemic illness needs medical evaluation.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003140.htm

## `sym.nocturia` — Nocturia / 夜尿
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: nocturia
- body_region: `urogenital`
- red_flag_note: New/worsening nocturia can reflect urinary, metabolic, cardiovascular or sleep disorders; assess context rather than treating it as a TCM-only finding.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003140.htm

## `sym.dysuria` — Dysuria / 小便灼熱 / 尿痛
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: dysuria
- body_region: `urogenital`
- red_flag_note: Dysuria with fever, flank pain, vomiting, pregnancy, gross hematuria or systemic illness is higher risk.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003140.htm

## `sym.scanty_urination` — Low urine output / 小便短少
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: oliguria / decreased urination
- body_region: `urogenital`
- red_flag_note: Markedly decreased urine output with dehydration, edema, dyspnea, hypotension or acute illness may indicate serious renal/hemodynamic disease.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003140.htm

## `sym.amenorrhea` — Amenorrhea / 閉經
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: amenorrhea
- body_region: `gyn_repro`
- red_flag_note: Pregnancy must be considered where applicable; primary amenorrhea after expected pubertal development or secondary amenorrhea with systemic symptoms warrants evaluation.
- sources:
  - https://www.medlineplus.gov/ency/article/001218.htm

## `sym.irregular_menses` — Irregular menses / 月經不調
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: irregular menstrual periods
- body_region: `gyn_repro`
- red_flag_note: Pregnancy, heavy/prolonged bleeding, syncope, severe pelvic pain or endocrine/systemic symptoms increase urgency.
- sources:
  - https://www.medlineplus.gov/ency/article/001218.htm

## `sym.loose_stool` — Loose stool / 便溏
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: loose stool / diarrhea spectrum
- body_region: `digestive`
- red_flag_note: Loose stools with blood/black stool, severe dehydration, high fever, severe pain or persistent vomiting need prompt assessment.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/diarrhea.html

## `sym.undigested_food_stool` — Visible undigested food in stool / 完穀不化
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: undigested food particles in stool
- body_region: `digestive`
- red_flag_note: Occasional visible food fragments can be benign; persistent change with weight loss, greasy stool, blood, severe diarrhea or malabsorption features needs evaluation.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/diarrhea.html

## `sym.dry_hard_stool` — Dry/hard stool / 大便乾結
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: hard dry stool / constipation
- body_region: `digestive`
- red_flag_note: Constipation with severe abdominal distension/pain, vomiting, inability to pass gas, blood/black stool or systemic illness is concerning.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/constipation.html

## `sym.irritability` — Irritability / 煩躁 / 易怒
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: irritability
- body_region: `sleep_energy_mood`
- red_flag_note: Abrupt severe behavioral change with confusion, mania/psychosis, suicidality, intoxication/withdrawal or neurologic signs requires urgent assessment.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003094.htm

## `sym.restlessness` — Restlessness / 煩躁不安
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: restlessness / agitation
- body_region: `sleep_energy_mood`
- red_flag_note: New severe agitation with altered mental status, fever, intoxication/withdrawal, neurologic deficits or danger to self/others is urgent.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003094.htm

## `sym.dream_disturbed_sleep` — Dream-disturbed sleep / 多夢
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: disturbing/vivid dreams with sleep disruption
- body_region: `sleep_energy_mood`
- red_flag_note: Persistent sleep disruption should be assessed for medication/substance effects, trauma/nightmares and sleep disorders; acute dangerous impairment is higher risk.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003094.htm

## `sym.sighing` — Frequent sighing / 善太息
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: frequent sighing / deep breaths
- body_region: `respiratory`
- red_flag_note: Frequent sighing may occur with stress or dysfunctional breathing, but chest pain, hypoxia, severe dyspnea or syncope requires urgent evaluation.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003094.htm

## `sym.globus_sensation` — Globus sensation / 梅核氣 / 咽中異物感
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: globus pharyngeus
- body_region: `ears_nose_throat`
- red_flag_note: Progressive dysphagia, odynophagia, weight loss, neck mass, bleeding or airway symptoms are alarm features.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/drymouth.html

## `sym.chest_tightness` — Chest tightness / 胸悶
- tradition: `both`
- western_term: chest tightness / chest pressure
- body_region: `chest_cardio`
- red_flag_note: New/severe chest pressure or tightness with dyspnea, sweating, nausea, syncope or radiation should be treated as potentially emergent.
- sources:
  - https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003094.htm

## Note
These red flags are safety prompts, not diagnostic inference rules. A selected symptom must never automatically create a Western Condition, TCM Disease, or TCM Pattern.
